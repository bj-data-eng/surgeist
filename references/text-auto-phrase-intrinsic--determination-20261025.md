# AutoPhrase overflow and intrinsic sizing

Source-only determination for #65/#66, 25 October 2026, against production
`6090645c3607cac221c795117fdb5d430a0388f7`. The Architecture Coordinator resolved
the intrinsic receipt choice from the selected sources. This records a contract,
not implementation or executed acceptance evidence.

## Decision

AutoPhrase retains the existing intrinsic Anywhere floor. Min-content uses
independently eligible, authentic TCU opportunities and real intrinsic
measurement; it requires no finite phrase-overflow receipt. Do not synthesize a
zero-width line or pretend an unconstrained phrase failed to fit.

Keep the ordinary phrase catalog intact. Normal and hyphen opportunities
restored during finite overflow do not enter min-content. An Anywhere cause at
the same endpoint remains independently eligible, without borrowing an
indication, continuation or relaxation authority. Normal/BreakWord intrinsic
queries cannot consume intrinsic-Anywhere authority. Max-content opens no
conditional stage.

Finite placement still restores Normal opportunities only after an actual
phrase overflows an otherwise empty line, then eligible owner hyphenation if
needed, then arbitrary effective overflow opportunities. An earlier intrinsic
query cannot unlock those finite stages. Ordinary AutoPhrase suppresses
hyphenation regardless of phrase-language support; the specified finite
restoration is a separate operation.

## Evidence and bounded policy

Selected [Text4 §6.4](css-text-4--WD-css-text-4-20260814--106c5cd086ab.md#overflow-wrap-property)
excludes AutoPhrase relaxation cuts from min-content, includes Anywhere cuts,
and excludes BreakWord arbitrary cuts. Its finite sequence does not prescribe
Surgeist's intrinsic receipt protocol. The current draft checked by the
Architecture Coordinator agrees with the retained 14 August 2026 edition.
[CSSWG #7193](https://github.com/w3c/csswg-drafts/issues/7193#issuecomment-1663101848)
establishes phrase fallback before overflow, without requiring a finite receipt
for intrinsic measurement.

Pinned [Blink SetCurrentStyleForce](https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src/+/daf70f9ddee4b45d4f8870f574fbde61c0db4ca7/third_party/blink/renderer/core/layout/inline/line_breaker.cc)
selects phrase mode and suppresses hyphens, then selects character breaking for
min-content with OverflowWrap Anywhere. BreakWord's arbitrary mode is restricted
to content layout. This is static mechanism evidence, not browser execution or
an interoperability claim.

The explicit bounded Surgeist choice is an intrinsic-only Anywhere path using
the existing genuine prepared-unit/measurement contract. Preserve intrinsic
query identity, actual wrap/owner/NCA eligibility, shaping continuity, source
correspondence and all mixed contributions. This is not minimum glyph advance,
glyph-count division or a monotonic-width assumption. Foreign, stale or
context-mismatched handles remain invalid.

## Acceptance

With wrapping and no other contributions or independent cuts:

| Actually qualified fixture | Normal / BreakWord min-content | Anywhere min-content | Max-content |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Unknown/unsupported phrase language, ABCD, four real Ahem advances of 10 | 40 | 10 | 40 |
| Supported Japanese, independently verified three-TCU phrase, admitted Japanese-capable fixed advances of 10 | 30 | 10 | 30 |

Retain declared language/style and the phrase/source catalog. Combining and
multi-scalar units remain intact. No restored Normal/hyphen offers or finite
receipt enter min-content, and shared endpoints gain only Anywhere's own cause.
No-wrap/NCA and mixed-owner restrictions remain effective. Finite placement must
still demonstrate its complete ordered relaxation after an intrinsic query.
