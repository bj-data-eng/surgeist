# IETF RFC 5646

The repository includes a [metadata-only source reference](../../references/bcp47-html-language-boundary--rfc5646.md) for *Tags for Identifying Languages*, RFC 5646, September 2009, edited by A. Phillips (Lab126) and M. Davis (Google). It includes no technical RFC excerpts or ABNF.

- Original notice: [RFC 5646 copyright and pre-November-2008 notice](COPYRIGHT-NOTICE.txt), copied verbatim from the RFC's first page.
- Homepage and definitive source: [RFC 5646](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5646.txt).
- Historic legal provisions: [TLP 2.0, effective February 15, 2009](https://trustee.ietf.org/wp-content/uploads/IETF-TLP-2.pdf) and [TLP 3.0, effective September 12, 2009](https://trustee.ietf.org/wp-content/uploads/IETF-TLP-3.pdf).

The RFC specifies the legal provisions in effect on its publication date and carries the pre-November-2008 material notice. Both historic TLP editions contain an attributed, unmodified-portions grant in §3.c.iii and Pre-5378 limitations in §5.c and §6.c.iii. The month-only publication marker does not establish which side of September 12 applies, and the selected portions have not been individually cleared against the pre-2008 limitation.
